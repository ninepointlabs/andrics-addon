New addon

The Priest's Companion Arrives for WoW Forever

Pick Holy, Discipline or Shadow and every page bends to it: what to cast, what to wear, where to quest and what to learn next.

From the presses of Nine Point Labs · for World of Warcraft: Forever · Interface 16001

A Priest has a great deal to keep in mind: which rank to train, which buff has fallen off, whether that robe in the bag is truly better, and what a healer does in Blackfathom Deeps when the tank pulls three packs. Andric's Addon keeps it in one window, sorted by what you should do next.

The Home page shows what to train, how much gold you have toward your riding, your next class quest and anything that needs attention. The Rotation page lists only the spells you can actually cast at your level, and an on-screen helper suggests the next one. Every item tooltip says whether the item is an upgrade for your spec and what it would change.

It was built for Forever alone. It reads Forever's own talent trees and supports dual spec (talent spec one and two each keep their own Priest profile). Where the sources disagree or say nothing, it says so instead of guessing.

In this edition

Home

What to do next, at a glance

Cards for your trainer, your savings toward riding at 40 and 60, and your next class quest. Below them: missing buffs, upgrades sitting in your bags, unread news and new lessons.

Rotation

Only spells you can cast

Priority lists for solo, dungeon and raid, filtered to your level and talents. An optional on-screen helper suggests the next spell for Shadow, or shows your priorities and cooldowns when healing, and each fight ends with a short summary and a mana tip. It gives advice only: nothing casts by itself.

Gear

Upgrade or not, and why

Item tooltips compare against what you wear for your spec, list what improves and what you'd lose, and say Equip this when it's worth it. Upgrades in your bags glow. For each recommended item the Gear page shows the boss and drop chance, the quest and quest giver, or the recipe, and can put a pin on your map.

Leveling

Your path to sixty

Upcoming trainer visits and milestones, zones and dungeons for your level and faction, every spell rank with the level you learn it, the racial class quests at ten and twenty, and your riding trainer and first mount.

Talents

Builds you can see

Recommended leveling, dungeon, raid and PvP builds drawn on Forever's real talent trees, with points per talent and in-game tooltips, plus the Forever talent changes for your tree.

Learn the Priest

A school in your spellbook

Every spell and talent explained (what it does, when to use it, which rank), how the class works, playing in a group, leveling each spec and common mistakes. New lessons unlock as you level, and the first time you learn a spell you get a short hint.

Fortitude

One button to buff them all

Each press casts Power Word: Fortitude on the nearest friendly player who doesn't have it yet: you first, then your group, your target, then players with friendly nameplates showing. You can bind it to any key.

Reminders & news

Nothing forgotten

Reminders for Fortitude, Inner Fire, Shadowform, your wand and candles. A What's new page collects Forever and Priest changes; an optional companion script on your PC keeps that list up to date.

Install with OmaForge

On Linux, one line:

omaforge install -c forever github:ninepointlabs/andrics-addon

Or search for andrics in OmaForge. Then /reload in game and type /andric.

By hand

Download the zip from the latest release and extract it into World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns, so that you have an AndricsAddon folder there.

WoW Forever only. Classic Era and retail clients won't load it.

Slash commands

  • /andric open or close
  • /priest same thing
  • /andric spec shadow holy · disc · shadow
  • /andric rotation gear · leveling · talents · learn
  • /andric fort key bind the Fortitude button
  • /andric remind check buffs now

Honest Data, Plainly Sourced

From the record

Spells, quests, builds, gear, riding costs and talent trees come from Blizzard's own notes and articles, Icy Veins and Wowhead. Every record keeps its source and the date it was checked, and each page has a Sources & notes link.

No guessing

Where the sources are silent or disagree (a cooldown here, a spell ID there, gear lists past level 30, raid best-in-slot before the raids open on December 9), the addon says so plainly rather than inventing an answer.

Kept current

The research is rechecked and the addon updated through new releases. Talent trees come from Forever's current client build, and the build string is checked whenever a build is drawn.

  1. Install with OmaForge or by hand, then /reload.
  2. Type /andric and pick Holy, Discipline or Shadow. With dual spec, pick one for each talent spec.
  3. Start on Home: it tells you what to do next.
