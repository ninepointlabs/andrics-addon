Quest Log

Everything Andric's Addon does for your Priest, one quest at a time.

Quest Log 0/9 accepted

    Know What to Do Next

    Every time you open the window, Home tells you what matters now: the spells waiting at your trainer, how close you are to your riding gold, your next class quest, and anything that needs attention, such as a missing buff, an upgrade in your bags or news you haven't read.

    Objectives

    • See what to train now and at your next trainer visit
    • Track your savings for riding at 40 (100g) and 60 (1000g)
    • Never miss your level 10 and 20 racial class quests

    Rewards

    Codex of Next Steps
    Codex of Next Steps Binds when installed Use: Shows what to do next, sorted by what matters. "Type /andric. Start on Home."

    Cast the Right Spell

    Priority lists for solo leveling, dungeons and raids, for each spec, filtered to the spells you can actually cast at your level. An optional on-screen helper suggests the next spell for Shadow, or shows your priorities and cooldowns while healing. After each fight you get a short summary and a mana tip. It only ever advises: nothing casts by itself.

    Objectives

    • Follow Solo, Dungeon and Raid priorities for Holy, Discipline and Shadow
    • See only spells your level and talents allow
    • Learn from a summary after every fight

    Rewards

    Whisper of the Next Cast
    Whisper of the Next Cast Unique Use: Suggests your next spell. You still press the button. "Mind Blast is ready."

    Fortitude for Everyone

    One small button on your screen. Each press casts Power Word: Fortitude on the nearest friendly player who doesn't have it yet: you first, then your group, your target, then players whose friendly nameplates are showing. It skips anyone who is already buffed, out of range or dead. Bind it to any key.

    Objectives

    • Buff the next player with a single press
    • Bind it to a key from Settings
    • Keep the whole town stamina-blessed

    Rewards

    Button of Endless Fortitude
    Button of Endless Fortitude Binds to a key Use: Fortitude for the nearest player without it. "One press per cast, as the Light intended."

    Upgrade or Vendor?

    Hover any item and the tooltip says whether it beats what you're wearing for your spec, what improves and what you'd lose, and Equip this when it's worth it. Upgrades sitting in your bags glow, and a new one gets a quick alert.

    Objectives

    • Compare every item with your equipped one, for your spec
    • See what each stat change means for your mana, crit and healing
    • Spot upgrades in your bags at a glance

    Rewards

    Appraiser's Robe
    Appraiser's Robe Chest · Cloth Equip: Tells you which robe is better, and why. "No separate upgrade addon needed."

    Where the Loot Drops

    The Gear page lists the best recommended item for each slot at your level. Click one to see who drops it and how often, which quest gives it and who starts that quest, or what it takes to craft. Press Mark on map to put a pin on the quest giver or the dungeon entrance.

    Objectives

    • Browse the best gear for your level, slot by slot
    • Find the boss, the quest giver or the recipe
    • Pin it on your map and follow the arrow

    Rewards

    Map of Likely Loot
    Map of Likely Loot Binds when installed Use: Marks the quest giver or dungeon entrance on your map.

    The Road to Sixty

    Your path laid out by level: upcoming trainer visits and milestones, zones and dungeons for your level and faction, every spell rank and when you learn it, the racial class quests, and your riding trainer and first mount.

    Objectives

    • Plan your next trainer visits
    • Find zones and dungeons for your level and faction
    • Complete your class quests and claim your first mount

    Rewards

    Reins of the Long Road
    Reins of the Long Road Requires level 40 Use: Reminds you how much gold you still need.

    A Build You Can See

    Recommended leveling, dungeon, raid and PvP builds for your spec, drawn on Forever's own talent trees with points per talent and the in-game tooltips. With dual spec, talent spec one and two each keep their own Priest profile, and the addon follows whichever you switch to.

    Objectives

    • See every recommended build on the real talent trees
    • Read what each build takes, talent by talent
    • Switch specs and let the addon follow

    Rewards

    Twin Tomes of Talent
    Twin Tomes of Talent Unique · Dual spec Use: Shows a build point by point on Forever's trees.

    Learn the Priest

    Every spell and talent explained: what it does, when to use it and which rank. Then how the class works, playing in a group, leveling each spec and the common mistakes. New lessons unlock as you level, and the first time you learn a spell you get a short hint.

    Objectives

    • Read about every spell and talent
    • Unlock lessons as you level
    • Learn your role in a group

    Rewards

    Tome of the Whole Priest
    Tome of the Whole Priest Soulbound Use: Teaches you everything there is about the class. "Unconfirmed facts are marked. The Light values honesty."

    Nothing Forgotten

    Reminders for Fortitude, Inner Fire, Shadowform, your wand and candles. What's new collects Forever and Priest changes, and an optional companion script on your PC keeps that list current. Every fact carries its source, and where the sources disagree or say nothing, the addon tells you instead of guessing.

    Objectives

    • Keep your buffs, wand and candles in order
    • Catch every Priest change in What's new
    • Check the source behind any fact

    Rewards

    Scroll of Honest Notes
    Scroll of Honest Notes Binds when installed Use: Shows where every fact came from.

    The Tavern

    Questions, answered by the innkeeper.

    Innkeeper Candlewick

    Light be with you, traveler! Warm your hands by the candles. What would you like to know?

    Does it work on Classic Era or retail?

    No. It's built for World of Warcraft: Forever only, and other clients won't load it. That's on purpose: Forever's spells, talents, quests and gear all differ from Classic.

    Will it play the game for me?

    No. Blizzard doesn't allow that, and it wouldn't be much fun. The rotation helper suggests, and the Fortitude button casts once per press. Everything else is advice and information.

    Which specs does it support?

    Holy, Discipline and Shadow. Pick one and every page follows it. With dual spec, talent spec one and two each keep their own profile, and the addon switches when you do.

    Where does the information come from?

    From Blizzard's own articles and development notes, Icy Veins and Wowhead. Every fact keeps its source and the date it was checked: look for the Sources & notes link on each page. Where the sources disagree or say nothing yet, such as gear lists past level 30 or raid gear before the raids open, the addon says so instead of guessing.

    How does What's new stay current?

    Addons can't go online from inside the game. New releases carry updated research, and an optional companion script on your PC can refresh the list in between: it reads public news feeds and writes a file the addon shows after a /reload.

    Do I need any other addons?

    No, it has no dependencies. Its bag highlight is drawn separately from Pawn's upgrade arrow, so the two don't overwrite each other.

    Is this made by Blizzard?

    No. Andric's Addon is an unofficial, free and open source fan project by Nine Point Labs, from the makers of My Azeroth Life and OmaForge. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.
